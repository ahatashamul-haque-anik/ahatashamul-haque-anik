Residential Rooftop PV System — 4.5 kWp (Dhaka)

System Design
Array size4.5 kWp (10 × 450W)
Inverter5 kW on-grid string
MountingTilt frame, 15°, south

Sized from a 15 kWh/day load at ~4.5 peak sun hours with a 20% system derate, giving ~4.2 kWp required — rounded to 10 × 450W panels (4.5 kWp). Roughly 30 m² of the 74 m² roof is used, leaving room for expansion.

Indicative economics (illustrative Bangladesh rates: ~80 BDT/W, ~9 BDT/kWh): ~৳360,000 installed cost, ~৳48,600/year savings, ~7–8 year payback.

Worked example for demonstration. Verify costs/tariffs with current vendor quotes, and confirm yield in PVSol or PVsyst before treating as final.
